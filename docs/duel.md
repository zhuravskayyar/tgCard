# Duel v1

Duel v1 is a server-authoritative fight against bots only. Every search creates a temporary bot snapshot with a generated human-like nickname; real players are never selected as opponents and bots are never inserted into `players`. Bot effective deck power is randomly selected inside ±10% of the challenger's power and is guaranteed to differ by at least 3%. The matchmaking range widens to ±15% when the current Duel win streak is at least five.

Starting a Duel freezes both sides' identity, level, nine card instances and final powers, artwork data, effective deck power, and shared starting HP. The bot receives nine distinct non-limited canonical card identities selected independently of the challenger's deck, a valid `3/2/2/2` element distribution, generated progression values whose final powers sum exactly to the selected bot power, zero collection modifiers, and synthetic instance identifiers. Each side is shuffled independently with server RNG exactly once. The persisted snapshot, three active slots, six-card reserve queues, HP, log, turn, and version are restored after reload; later inventory changes cannot alter the battle.

The board has three mirrored pairs. The challenger selects only one own slot, and the opposing card in that slot always counterattacks in the same exchange. Both attacks are calculated before either result is evaluated. Damage uses the canonical `0.5 / 1 / 1.5` element matrix, card instance `finalPower`, global battle damage, and attacker-element damage modifiers. Shared HP uses effective deck power plus the battle HP modifier and is clamped at zero.

After every exchange, the used card on each side moves to the back of its reserve queue and the front reserve card replaces only that active slot. The nine-card pool is cyclic and never runs out. If both shared HP pools reach zero in the same completed exchange, the initiating challenger wins.

Every resolved exchange appends one immutable log snapshot containing the exact two cards, both multipliers, and both damage values. Clients submit only `slotIndex` and `expectedVersion`; stale actions return `duel_state_conflict` and cannot resolve or reward twice.

Finalization is one transaction. A win increments wins and the current streak; a loss increments losses and resets the streak. A win grants XP equal to the defeated opponent's starting HP (overkill damage is not XP); a loss grants no XP. Both outcomes grant Silver, including completed collection reward modifiers. Account XP requires `currentLevel × 100` per next level and preserves overflow. Duel Gold follows the reference manual: the daily limit is the account level plus completed collection bonuses (`dragons` +5, `dragons2` +10), doubled by the currency boost. Gold drops on a random win (40% chance) and always by the 5th finished Duel, losses included, since the last drop (`players.duel_gold_streak`). A drop is about 5% of the daily limit (10% with `umutants`), rounded up or down at random, at least 1 and never above what is left. Losses never pay Gold. The counter resets only with the next UTC day; a level increase raises the limit to the new level but keeps the Gold already earned today (as in the reference manual). Level-up Gold is separate, does not count toward this limit, and is granted for every level crossed by one reward.

Manual Duels use ten charges. A normal Duel consumes one charge when its battle starts, regardless of outcome; the tutorial Duel does not consume one. The tenth charge starts a two-hour cooldown, reduced to 90 minutes by the completed `highelem` collection. Once it expires, availability reads as ten charges and the next normal start persists the refreshed count. The server checks availability again under the player row lock when starting a battle, so a stale search cannot bypass the limit. Gold bonus Duels for card upgrades are not implemented yet.

## Paid and automatic Duels

Rules follow the reference manual (`/forum/3/217686/`); code: `game-core/src/duelPaid.ts`, `server/src/duel/duelCooldown.ts` (`getDuelPaidPrices`), `DuelService.resetBreak` / `autoPlay`, routes `POST /api/duel/reset` and `POST /api/duel/auto` (`{ mode: "remaining" | "pack" }`), migration 090.

- During the break gold either reopens ten manual Duels (`reset`; the break timer is cleared) or plays ten Duels automatically (`pack`). Both cost the same.
- The base price is 25 gold, then 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1000: one step per 1000 automatic Duels played today (`players.duel_auto_count` / `duel_auto_day`, UTC day). The price is scaled by the break time left over the full break (90 minutes with `highelem`) and rounded up, never below 1: 50 × 72 / 120 = 30, 25 × 3 / 90 → 1.
- After a won Duel with charges left, the result screen asks «Пройти решту дуелей?» with a button «N дуелей за N» (1 gold per Duel, `remaining`). The server checks it too: the latest non-tutorial bot Duel must be a win and fewer than ten charges must remain. These Duels also count toward the 1000 step; their own price stays flat.
- Automatic Duels are real finished Duels: a fresh bot per fight, the same exchange rules, rewards, gold drops, league, guild activity and campaign events as a manual Duel, each with its own row in `duels`. Our assumption (the reference does not say): every turn takes the pair with the best trade, own hit minus the return hit (`chooseAutoDuelSlot`).
- After any automatic run the charges are 0 and a new break starts.
- Known gap: the reference starts the break counter with the first Duel of a cycle; ours starts it at the tenth. This only shifts the proportional price.
