# elem.mobi: guild rules

Our own summary of «Мануал: гильдия» (`https://elem.mobi/forum/3/229391/`, signed-in account),
read on 2026-11-12. Mechanics only.

## Founding

- Level 10, not in another guild; costs 10 000 silver.
- Unique name (one alphabet, up to 20 characters, at most 2 spaces), one of 4 elements, one of 10 crests; none can change later.
- The element only sets the guild card's element and the crest colour.

## Guild XP and level

## Treasury

- Members who have been in the guild at least three days may contribute.
- Silver: unlimited. Gold: at most 5 a day per player, the limit does not accumulate; «Редкие драконы» raise it to 10.
- The limit also grows once by the amount of purchased gold (with its standing gifts, not promotional extras); the daily part is used first.
- «Генералы Урфина»: the game adds 20% to gold and silver contributions.
- Treasury gold is spent only by the leader or deputy on gold levels of the guild card and dragon caves; silver only on caves.

## Statistics, alliances, chronicle

- Five per-player stats: XP, battle XP, guild card elements, treasury gold, treasury silver; lost when the player leaves.
  Global and current (resettable by leader/deputy) versions.
- Alliances of two guilds: shared chat and the ally's guild card as an extra card (power × own loyalty, ≤ 100%).
- Announcements up to 255 characters by archmage rank and above.
- Chronicle groups: staff, treasury, raids, achievements.

## Achievements

- «Золото в казне»: 100 / 10K / 50K gold. «Улучшение карты гильдии»: 50K / 500K / 5M elements.
- Dragons in raids 25 / 250 / 1000; keys in wars 25 / 150 / 300; war battles 15 / 100 / 200;
  guild XP in one guild 30M / 3G / 30G.
