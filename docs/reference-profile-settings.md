# Reference: profile settings (elem.mobi/settings/)

Observed on 2026-11-11 from the reference account. Mechanics only; our visual style stays our own.

Entry: a «Настройки» link at the bottom of the player's own profile. The header shows avatar, name, level and guild. Then five buttons:

1. **Анкета** (`/settings/form/`)
   - Change name: costs 500 (premium currency), with a "remember the rules" note.
   - Gender: male or female.
   - Birthday: day and month only, with an "expensive to change" warning.
2. **Выбрать аватар** (`/settings/avatar/`)
   - The avatar is the art of any card from the player's collections.
   - Element filter tabs (fire / water / earth / air) above a 3-column grid of card thumbnails.
   - One tap applies it (`/settings/avatar/use/<card_id>/`). «Вернуться без изменения» leaves it unchanged.
3. **Выбрать облик** (`/settings/silhouette/`)
   - The full-body profile silhouette, the same thing as our `ProfileMage`.
   - Owned silhouettes are listed with an «Установить» button; locked ones show a padlock.
   - «Купить новый облик или силуэт» links to the shop.
4. **Оформление** (`/settings/decor/`)
   - Three separate forms, each with its own «Сохранить»:
     - profile frame theme (radio, "Стандарт");
     - battle screen design (radio: old / new);
     - duel animation on/off (checkbox).
5. **Изменить пароль**: account security; not relevant to Telegram login.
