What is Cardastika

Cardastika (in Ukrainian Кардастіка, also spelled Кардастика) is a free fantasy card game set in the world of the World Tree. It runs right in your browser and in Telegram: nothing to download, and you can start without registering.

How to play

Build a nine-card deck and field three cards at a time. Every card belongs to one of four elements: Fire, Water, Air or Earth. Elements have advantages over each other, so the player who picks the right cards wins.

  • Duels against other players and the arena
  • A campaign with adventures and bosses
  • Card collections and upgrades
  • Guilds, raids and tournaments

Where to play

Open Cardastika on your phone or computer in any modern browser, or as a Telegram Mini App. Link your progress to a Google or Telegram account so you never lose it.
