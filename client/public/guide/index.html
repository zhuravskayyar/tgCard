How to Play Cardastika

Rules, elements, decks and tips for new players.

The game in short

Cardastika is a free fantasy card game set in the world of the World Tree. You collect creature and hero cards, build them into a deck, fight duels, clear a campaign full of bosses and play together with a guild. It opens in any modern browser on phone or computer, and as a Telegram Mini App.

A nine-card deck

Your battle deck always holds nine cards and covers all four elements: one element is represented by three cards and the other three by two each (a 3/2/2/2 split). You never have to arrange it by hand: the game automatically fields the strongest cards you own while keeping that balance. To make your deck stronger, simply earn and upgrade cards.

Four elements

Every card belongs to Fire, Water, Air or Earth. Elements have advantages over each other: a favourable matchup deals one and a half times the damage, an unfavourable one only half. In battle it matters not only how strong a card is, but which card you send it against.

How a battle works

Three cards face each other on each side of the board. You choose which card attacks, and the opposing card in the same position strikes back at once. After the exchange both used cards go to the back of their queues and the next cards from the deck take their place. The first side to bring the opponent's shared health to zero wins.

Game modes

  • Duels. Quick fights for silver, gold and experience. A win grants experience equal to the defeated opponent's health.
  • Campaign. Ten locations with trials and bosses. The first victory over a boss rewards gold, silver and a rare card.
  • Arena, leagues and tournaments. Compete with other players for ranking places.
  • Guilds. Shared raids, guild wars and bonuses for members.

Cards and collections

The game has more than two hundred cards across six rarity levels, most of them grouped into fifty collections. A completed collection gives a permanent bonus, for example to silver or to the gold you can earn from duels. New cards come from the campaign, the shop, chests and daily rewards.

Tips for new players

  • Log in every day: daily finds give cards, equipment, gold and experience, and day seven brings an Epic card.
  • Push through the campaign: bosses are the most reliable source of strong cards early on.
  • Watch the elements in battle and attack with the pair where your card has the advantage.
  • Join a guild to earn shared rewards.
  • Link a Google or Telegram account so you never lose your progress.

FAQ

Is Cardastika free?

Yes. The game is free, runs in your browser and in Telegram, and needs no download.

Do I need to register?

No. You can start right away and link your progress to a Google or Telegram account later.

How is the name written?

Cardastika; in Ukrainian Кардастіка, also spelled Кардастика.
