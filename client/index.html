Cardastika (Кардастіка) — Online Fantasy Card Game

Cardastika (Кардастіка, also written Кардастика) is a free game set in the world of the World Tree. Play in your browser or Telegram without downloading: start a new journey without registering or return to your saved account.

Build a nine-card deck, field three cards at a time, and command Fire, Water, Air and Earth. Use elemental advantages and choose your cards to win duels.

Discover new cards, complete collections, explore campaign adventures and join a guild. Develop your deck and discover the fantasy world at your own pace.
