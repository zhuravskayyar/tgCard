# Archived starter card artwork

These files were served from `client/public/card-art/` for the retired
`starter_01`–`starter_09` catalog. They are kept here for recovery and are no
longer served by the Mini App.

The active catalog is `starter_v5_01`–`starter_v5_09`; its artwork remains in
`client/public/card-art/new-cards/`. Legacy card definitions and player-state
snapshots are retained in the local database by migration
`062_archive_legacy_starter_cards.sql`.
